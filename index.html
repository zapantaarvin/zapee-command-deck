02 · Process

Operational Workflow

The 10-step pipeline every Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) moves through, per the SOP's (Standard Operating Procedure) Daily Operating Procedures (Section 10). Wrapped into two rows so it stays readable. Raymond runs the front half solo, Arlene runs the back half hands-on, Arvin owns Reporting only. Diamonds are approval/decision gates — these stay with Arlene and Arvin regardless of who does the hands-on step. Click any step for its full procedure — good for walking the team through one at a time.

Procedure 01 — Product Sourcing & Supplier Communication

Trigger
Low stock alert or new product research
Frequency
As needed / monthly review
Records
Supplier quotes, screenshots, notes
  1. Identify sourcing need: new product, restock, or better supplier
  2. Research suppliers on Lazada, Shopee wholesale, Alibaba, or direct contacts
  3. Contact minimum 2–3 suppliers per product for price comparison
  4. Request MOQ, unit price, shipping cost, and lead time
  5. Save quotes in the Supplier Tracker
  6. Propose preferred supplier to Arlene with cost comparison summary
  7. Receive Arlene's approval before placing any order
KPI: 3+ supplier quotes compared per product. Sourcing cost vs. target price within 20%.
Escalation: Supplier fails to deliver twice in a row → raise to Arlene for a replacement decision.

Overhead — What's New

Most of this already had a home in the SOP's existing lines — the real gap was narrower than it looked.

Utilities, phone, internet, transportation

Not new top-level deductions — fold into the existing "Miscellaneous expenses" line with explicit rules, since Zapee is home-based with no dedicated leased space yet:

  • Transportation: reimbursed only for supplier visits, bank runs, or DTI/BIR/LGU (Department of Trade and Industry / Bureau of Internal Revenue / Local Government Unit) errands — receipt required
  • Phone/internet: a flat 30–40% partial allocation per person, since these lines carry both personal and seller-app/CX use — not the full bill
  • Utilities: not material yet — revisit only once Zapee has a dedicated packing space with its own meter

Labor, if hiring outside the three of you

This is the one genuinely new gap — nothing in the current formula accounts for paying a non-principal. Two sub-types, since they behave differently:

  • Direct labor (e.g., a packer paid per parcel) — variable, scales with order volume
  • Admin/support labor (e.g., a virtual assistant on monthly retainer) — fixed, same every month regardless of sales

Not material today, but worth watching — Raymond solo-running Sourcing through Selling is a lot for one person.

Reinvestment Strategy
Recommended: 20% of net profit, separate from the Reserve

The existing 15%/₱5,000-minimum Reserve (SOP Section 6.1–6.2) is sized purely for downside protection — fee spikes, urgent restocking, emergency refunds. It was never meant to fund growth. Section 6.3 already lists six reinvestment priorities (restocking, ads, packaging/branding, software, product expansion, emergency buffer) but never actually funds them — right now they'd compete with the emergency reserve for money, which is the wrong pool.

General small-business benchmark, not Zapee-specific data (there's no live revenue yet to calibrate against): a commonly cited range for an early-stage, inventory-heavy e-commerce seller is 20–30% of net profit reinvested during active growth. Given Zapee is still pre-launch, the conservative end — 20% — is the sensible starting point, revisited once real Day 50-70 data exists.

Salary vs. 40/30/30 Split
Opt-in salary alternative (SOP 5.5–5.6) — already exists, individual, per quarter

Arvin or Raymond may each individually elect a fixed monthly salary instead of their profit share for a given quarter. Arlene's 40% is not eligible — she's the capital/legal owner, not an opt-in labor partner. This isn't a new mechanism; the real question is just whether opting in makes sense right now given the numbers.

Existing reference ranges: Arvin ₱25,000–35,000/mo · Raymond ₱12,000–18,000/mo.

Direct answer: overhead shrinks the size of Net Distributable Profit — it doesn't change the 40/30/30 ratio. So overhead by itself isn't a reason to prefer salary; it only matters if the shrunk pool now regularly pays a person less than their fixed-wage alternative. That's what break-even below tests.

Appendix J — Order Packaging Checklist
  • Correct item picked (matches order product code)
  • Correct quantity confirmed
  • Item inspected — no defects, damage, or missing parts
  • Zapee World branding / label applied correctly
  • Item protected (bubble wrap, filler if fragile)
  • Outer packaging sealed properly — no gaps or loose seals
  • Shipping label printed and attached correctly
  • Customer name and address readable on label
  • Order number on the package or packing slip inside
  • Photo of packed parcel taken before handoff
  • Tracking number recorded
  • Order marked "shipped" on platform
  • Inventory count updated

Customer Service Standards

Response targets: Shopee/Lazada chat ≤1h, TikTok messages ≤2h, returns ≤24h, disputes ≤4h. Operating hours 8am–8pm daily.

Tone: Filipino/Taglish unless the buyer writes in English. Friendly, solution-focused, never argue.

Escalation: Raymond handles general inquiries and refunds under ₱500 directly; ₱500–2,000 escalates to Arvin; above ₱2,000 or buyer threats escalate to Arlene.

Exit, Breach & Confidentiality
  • 14 consecutive days unnotified absence = inactive, forfeits that period's share
  • Voluntary exit: 30 days' written notice, settlement within 30 days after last active day
  • Forced removal grounds: unauthorized fund transfer, credential misuse, deliberate harm to supplier/customer relationships, confidential info disclosure
  • Confidentiality obligations survive 2 years past exit
